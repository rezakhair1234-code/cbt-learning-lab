Module 03

Cognitive Restructuring

The goal is not to replace negative thoughts with positive ones. It is to examine whether an appraisal is accurate, useful and sufficiently supported by evidence.

Core principle

Accuracy > positivity.

Clinical sequence

  1. Identify the situation and the hot automatic thought.
  2. Clarify emotion and intensity.
  3. Examine evidence supporting and contradicting the thought.
  4. Generate a balanced alternative that accounts for the evidence.
  5. Re-rate emotion and decide whether further assessment is needed.
